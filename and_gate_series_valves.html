🚰 Water Pipes System (Series)

❌ Both Valves OFF:

• First valve (A) is CLOSED - water blocked at Valve A
• Second valve (B) is CLOSED - water blocked at Valve B
• No water reaches output 🚫
• OUTPUT = OFF (No Water Flow)

⚠️ Series Connection Key Point:
Water must pass through Valve A AND THEN through Valve B to reach the output. If ANY valve is closed, the entire flow is blocked!

⚡ Transistor Circuit (AND Gate)

❌ Both Inputs LOW (OFF):

• Transistor A is OFF - blocks current
• Transistor B is OFF - blocks current
• No current reaches output ⚡
• OUTPUT = LOW (0V / OFF)

⚠️ Series Connection Key Point:
Current must flow through Transistor A AND THEN through Transistor B. If EITHER transistor is OFF, the current path is broken!

🚰 AND Gate = Two Valves in Series

Think of two security checkpoints in a hallway, one after another:

  • ✅ Both doors open? → You can walk through! (Output ON)
  • ❌ First door closed? → Blocked at checkpoint A (Output OFF)
  • ❌ Second door closed? → Blocked at checkpoint B (Output OFF)
  • ❌ Both doors closed? → Blocked at both! (Output OFF)

🔑 Key Insight: BOTH valves/transistors must be ON for anything to flow through!
