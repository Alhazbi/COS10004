🚿 NOT Gate: Shower Valve Analogy

Understanding How Transistors Invert Signals Using Water Flow

🚿 Shower System Analogy

🔵 INPUT OFF (Valve Handle Up):

• Valve is CLOSED - water cannot reach drain
• Water pressure builds up in pipe
• Water flows to showerhead (OUTPUT ON) 💦
• OUTPUT = HIGH PRESSURE (ON)

⚡ Transistor Circuit (NOT Gate)

🔵 INPUT OFF (0V / LOW):

• Transistor is OFF - blocks current to ground
• Current cannot flow down to ground
• Current flows to OUTPUT instead ⚡
• OUTPUT = HIGH VOLTAGE (ON / 1)

🔄 Analogy Mapping

🏊
Water Tank ➜ Power Supply
Constant source of pressure/voltage (+5V)
🚰
Drain ➜ Ground
Where water/current flows away (0V reference)
🎚️
Valve ➜ Transistor
Controls the flow path (ON = conducting, OFF = blocked)
🖐️
Valve Handle ➜ Input Signal
Control signal (HIGH opens valve, LOW closes valve)
🚿
Showerhead ➜ Output
Where you want water/current to flow
