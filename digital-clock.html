⚡ How Digital Watches Work

Digital watches use a quartz crystal that vibrates at exactly 32,768 Hz (2¹⁵). This specific frequency is chosen because 15 binary counter stages (toggle flip-flops) can divide it down to exactly 1 Hz — one tick per second.

Each divide-by-2 stage is a single toggle flip-flop. The output toggles every time the input goes high, effectively halving the frequency. Chain 15 of these together and you transform 32,768 rapid pulses into 1 slow tick per second!
