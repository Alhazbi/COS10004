How It Works

The Ripple Effect

Each flip-flop toggles on the falling edge of its input. The output of each stage becomes the clock for the next, creating a "ripple" through the chain.

Binary Counting

Q₀ toggles every clock cycle, Q₁ every 2 cycles, Q₂ every 4, and Q₃ every 8. This creates a 4-bit binary count from 0 to 15.

Propagation Delay

Since each stage triggers the next, there's a small cumulative delay. This is why it's called "asynchronous" — not all bits change simultaneously.
