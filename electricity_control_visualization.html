💧⚡ Controlling Electricity

COS10004 - Computer Systems | Understanding Switches and Flow Control

🎯 Key Concept: Electricity Can Be Controlled

  • Water Analogy: Just like using a tap to open or close a water pipe to control water flow exactly how you want
  • Circuit Design: We can design circuits to turn electricity ON or OFF
  • Voltage Control: We can increase or decrease the voltage
  • How? Using Switches to control the flow: ON (1) or OFF (0)

🔑 The Parallel: Just as a tap controls water flow in a pipe, a switch controls electrical current in a circuit. Both can be fully OPEN (ON/1), fully CLOSED (OFF/0), or partially controlled to regulate the flow rate/voltage.

💧 Water Tap Control

Physical mechanism:

  • Turn tap handle → valve opens
  • Water flows through pipe
  • Adjust opening → control flow rate
  • Close tap → water stops
  • Result: Precise control over water delivery

🔌 Switch Control

Simple electrical control:

  • Flip switch → circuit closes
  • Electricity flows through wire
  • ON = 1 (high voltage)
  • OFF = 0 (no voltage)
  • Result: Binary control of current

🎯 Why This Matters

Foundation of computing:

  • ON/OFF states = binary digits (bits)
  • Billions of tiny switches working together
  • Control flow at nanosecond speeds
  • Result: Complex computations from simple switches
