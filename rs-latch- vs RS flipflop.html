🔑 Key Differences

🔓 Latch (Transparent)

Changes output immediately when inputs change. Like an open gate - signals pass through instantly.

🔒 Flip-Flop (Synchronized)

Changes output only on clock edge. Like a turnstile - only lets signals through at specific moments.

💡 Why It Matters

Flip-flops enable synchronous circuits where all components update together, preventing race conditions and glitches.
