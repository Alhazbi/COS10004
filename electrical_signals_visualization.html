🎯 What is an Electrical Signal?

An electrical signal is a time-varying quantity (voltage or current) that represents information. Digital circuits use two distinct voltage levels to represent binary states (0 and 1), while analog signals vary continuously.

📈 Analog Signals

Characteristics:

  • Vary continuously over time
  • Can take any value in a range
  • Represent real-world data accurately
  • Sensitive to noise and distortion
  • Examples: Sound waves, temperature sensors

🔲 Digital Signals

Characteristics:

  • Have discrete levels (0 and 1)
  • 0 = Low voltage (typically 0V-0.5V)
  • 1 = High voltage (typically 2.8V-3.3V)
  • Resistant to noise
  • Easy to store and transmit
  • Used in computers and digital systems

💡 Why Binary?

Computers use binary because:

  • Transistors have 2 states: ON/OFF
  • Reliable and less error-prone
  • Easy to implement in digital circuits
  • Simple logic operations (AND, OR, NOT)
  • Foundation of all digital computing
