🔄 Synchronous Counters

All flip-flops share the same clock - no ripple, no illegal states!

⚡ THE KEY DIFFERENCE

In synchronous counters, ALL flip-flops receive the SAME clock signal.
They all change at the exact same moment — no ripple effect, no illegal states!

⚖️ Ripple vs Synchronous
🌊 Ripple Counter
  • ❌ Each FF clocked by previous FF's output
  • ❌ Bits change at different times
  • ❌ Illegal intermediate states exist
  • ❌ Cumulative propagation delay
  • ✅ Simpler circuit, fewer gates
⚡ Synchronous Counter
  • ✅ All FFs share the same clock
  • ✅ All bits change simultaneously
  • ✅ NO illegal states!
  • ✅ Single propagation delay
  • ❌ Needs AND gates for toggle logic
