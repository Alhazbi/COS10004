💡 The Key Difference: Clock Edge Triggering

⬆️ UP = Falling Edge ▼ Triggered

All flip-flops trigger on FALLING edges ▼.
• Q₀ toggles when CLK falls
• Q₁ toggles when Q₀ falls
Sequence: 00 → 01 → 10 → 11 → 00

⬇️ DOWN = Rising Edge ▲ Triggered

All flip-flops trigger on RISING edges ▲.
• Q₀ toggles when CLK rises
• Q₁ toggles when Q₀ rises
Sequence: 00 → 11 → 10 → 01 → 00
